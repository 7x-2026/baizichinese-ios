The Drum

The drum held a very high status in primitive society. People would beat drums for enjoyment or during rituals. Even on battlefields, drums were used from the very beginning. When the war drum sounded, one of the Five Emperors, Zhuanyu, demanded that the musicians play music. The musician, Shang, lay down and beat his own belly. Ku also invented the drum. He covered a large barrel with deer skin and beat it while striking a stone to imitate the jade bells in the heavenly palace. Many stories exist about the ancient musician Ku inventing the drum. Ku looked like a bull, with a broad body and no horns. His people lived in the forests among the mountains. Ku listened carefully to the songs of morning birds, the calls of insects at night, the wind, the rain, the rustling of leaves, and the gentle flow of mountain streams. He carefully listened and felt the music hidden within these different sounds. Eventually, he created his own music. His music attracted hundreds of birds. Later, animals in the forest gathered together and danced to the rhythm of the music—a truly unprecedented grand scene.

Later, to imitate this magnificent scene, people began to wear feathers, animal skins, and animal tails and dance to the music.

The Yellow Emperor learned of Kuai's musical talent and asked Kuai to come to his palace to perform. Kuai sang with a voice full and joyful, and he also beat a large drum covered with animal skin, striking stone pieces to recreate the music of a festival. The music, especially the drumbeat, delighted the Yellow Emperor. The ten tribes often fought with one another, and whenever a drum was beaten on the battlefield, the soldiers' morale soared and they could overcome any obstacle. Once, the Yellow Emperor decided to attack the rebel Chiyou. The battle was fierce, and realizing his own fate, Kuai fell to his knees and wept. People asked him why he was crying. He replied, "The Yellow Emperor is fighting a war." They found this ridiculous and said, "The Yellow Emperor is fighting Chiyou, not anyone else." At that moment, Kuai replied, "War requires a war drum, and the Yellow Emperor will surely use my skin to make one." Indeed, one night, the Yellow Emperor ordered people to kill Kuai and use his skin to make a war drum. During battles, both sides would beat the war drum. When the drum was beaten again, the sound shook the sky, and people could hear it as far as five hundred miles away, greatly boosting the morale of the Yellow Emperor's army.

Chiyou had eighty-one brothers, with copper heads and iron arms, and vowed to kill no matter what. Yet, despite his eight-armed strength and invulnerability to weapons, he could not stand against the army of the Yellow Emperor, which carried war drums. In the end, the army of the Yellow Emperor defeated and killed Chiyou. After Ku died, he went to the East Sea and became the Thunder God. His voice sounded like a war drum, and whenever it was heard, all demons and monsters would step aside.
