Xiaochen and the Dragon Brothers — A First-Grade Play

Actors:

Xiaochen – the boy who herds ducks

Yellow Dragon (played by 3 actors)

Purple Dragon (played by 3 actors)

Liu Sanjie

Two woodpeckers

One snake (played by 2 actors)

One tiger (played by 2 actors)

One eagle (played by 2 actors)

Narrator: 5 actors

Flute and singing: 5 actors

Narrator: Long ago, there was a moon-shaped mountain.

On that moon-shaped mountain stood a cave called Long Yin Cave.

Dragons once lived in that cave.

A stone trail winds clearly visible.

In the village lived a boy named Xiaochen.

His family was poor and he earned his living by herding ducks.

Every year, he herded ducks along the Li River.

Every day, he passed by Long Yin Cave.

Today, something strange happened.

Two people seemed to be sitting inside the cave.

Xiaochen climbed up the rock cave to find out what was going on.

There were two brothers playing chess.

Day after day, they remained seated there.

Xiaochen felt very curious.

He climbed into the mountain cave again to see what was happening.

One yellow and one purple dragon sat motionless at their desks.

They didn’t eat or drink — it had been three days.

Suddenly, a song came from outside the mountain.

Liu Sanjie rode on a cloud and floated into Guilin:

Liu Sanjie: Long ago, there was a crescent mountain,  
Inside the cave, two rain dragons were lazy and idle,  
They only played chess and did nothing else,  
Why didn’t they go up to heaven and bring the rain?

Narrator: The two brothers stopped playing chess and sighed,  
Feeling sad, they finally told the truth:  

Dragon Brothers: We are the two dragons in the song,  
We loved the mountains and rivers, and missed the flood season,  
We stayed in the cave pretending to play chess,  
But in our hearts, we longed to go back to heaven and bring the rain.  

Xiaochen: If you truly want to go to heaven and bring rain,  
Why are you still staying here?  

Purple Dragon: We are too close to the villagers,  
If we fly up, we’ll cause sudden storms and heavy rain,  
The villagers’ houses will be flooded,  
And mountain floods will destroy the farmland.  

Xiaochen: Dragon brothers, you both can swim,  
Why not swim down the small stream to the middle of the river,  
Then fly from the Li River to the sky?  
That way, you won’t harm the villagers or the people.  

Yellow Dragon: How could we not want to do that?

But the little stream is too narrow to cross.

There's one thing you can help with,  
but it's too young for you.  

Xiaochen: I'm already seven years old.  
I know the terrain here very well.  
Tell me what I can do.  
I'll do everything to help you.  

Yellow Dragon: To the northeast there's a mountain called Yao Mountain.  
Inside the rock wall on top of the mountain, there are two sacred bamboo poles.  
Cut them down and put them into the Li River.  
The river level will rise immediately.

Narrator: After hearing Yellow Dragon's words,

Xiaochen immediately rushed to climb the mountain.

The Dragon Brothers quickly stepped forward to stop him.

They pulled Xiaochen back and told the truth:

Dragon Brothers: Xiaochen, Xiaochen, you're too young.

To complete this task, you must pass three trials:

First, Yao Mountain is very high—you can't climb it.

Second, wild beasts roam freely—you'll go and never return.

Third, magic bamboo is extremely hard—you can't cut it.

Besides these three trials, there are many other difficulties.

Xiaochen: Even if Yao Mountain is tall, I will climb it.

Even if the beasts are fierce, I won't be stopped.

Even if the magic bamboo is hard, I will cut it.

I will pass through the three trials and overcome all difficulties.

Please watch over my ducks.

I'll finish this task quickly and return right away.

Narrator: With a flatbread and a chopping knife,

Xiaochen has already set off on his journey.

He crossed the river and entered the forest,

Suddenly, two woodpeckers flew in from ahead.

Woodpeckers: It hasn't rained for days—there's a severe drought.

No crops can grow, and tree insects have no place to hide.

Please help us—we haven't eaten for three days.

Narrator: Xiaochen didn't even think twice.

He gave the flatbread to the woodpeckers.

He kept walking for another whole day.  
Finally, he reached the foot of Yao Mountain.  
Just as he sat down to catch his breath,  
a giant snake extended its tongue and attacked.  
Xiaochen dodged the snake's attack.  
Faced with danger, he remained incredibly calm.  

Xiaochen: Snake brothers, snake brothers,  
eating me would only be one meal for you.  
Let me reach the top of the mountain and find the sacred bamboo—  
only then can I help the dragons go to heaven and stop the rain.  
The sky is dry, the land is thirsty, and all living things suffer.  
I can help ease the suffering of all beings—please let me pass.  

(After hearing this, the snake slowly retreated.)  

Narrator: Thorns from the thorny bushes wounded Xiaochen's hands and feet.  
It wasn't until nightfall that he managed to climb to the mountain's middle slope.  
His back ached and his waist hurt—he just sat down.  
Suddenly, a tiger leaped out from the tree line.  
Xiaochen dodged the tiger's attack.  
Faced with danger, he remained incredibly calm.  

Xiaochen: Tiger brothers, tiger brothers,  
eating me would only be one meal for you.  
Let me reach the top of the mountain and find the sacred bamboo—  
only then can I help the dragons go to heaven and stop the rain.  
The sky is dry, the land is thirsty, and all living things suffer.  
I can help ease the suffering of all beings—please let me pass.  

(After hearing this, the tiger slowly retreated.)  

Narrator: Xiaochen felt extremely hungry in his stomach.

Eating wild strawberries and climbing the mountain again

On the third morning, as the sun rose,  
he finally reached the top of Yao Mountain  

Two magic bamboo shoots grew from the rock crevices  
the bamboo stems were crisp, the leaves lush and green  

Xiaochen excitedly came to the bamboo,  
lifted his axe and chopped at the bamboo  

A giant eagle circled down,  
stretching out its sharp beak and claws toward Xiaochen  

Xiaochen dodged the eagle's attack  
facing danger, he remained unusually calm  

Xiaochen: Brother Eagle, brother Eagle,  
eating me would only be a meal for one day  
I climbed to the mountain top to find the divine bamboo  
so I can help the dragons go up and stop the rain  
the sky is dry, the land is thirsty,  
all living things suffer,  
I must solve the people's suffering—please let me pass  

(After hearing these words, the eagle flew back into the sky)  

Narrator: Xiaochen chopped hard, shaking the bamboo as he cut  
he split one crack, then it closed again  
two woodpeckers flew in to help  
with their sharp beaks, they pecked at the bamboo roots  

Finally, the magic bamboo fell over  

Xiaochen held his hands together and thanked the woodpeckers  

Xiaochen: Thank you!

The kind brothers  

Without you, I could not obtain the sacred bamboo.  
I will go down to help my dragon brothers reach the sky.  
As soon as the dragon goes up to heaven, rain will fall.  

Narrator: The path down the mountain takes two days and two nights.  
On the fifth night, they arrive at the foot of the Crescent Mountain.  
The bamboo is brought to the entrance of Long Hidden Cave.  
Two dragon brothers come to welcome them.  

Dragon Brothers: You have brilliantly completed your mission.  
Hurry and go up to the cliff wall.  
A small stream will flow through the Li River.  
Before the sun rises, we will meet again.  

Narrator: Xiaochen climbs up the cliff wall and looks down at the Li River.  
Dark clouds cover the stars for a whole night.  
Suddenly, a strong wind blows and uproots the trees.  
In the darkness, a flash of light appears.  
Through the flash, the river water rises.  
A tremendous roar echoes as waves crash against the cliff.  
Two bamboo poles are now placed into the river.  
Two rain dragons have swum close to the Li River.  

Xiaochen: Dragon brothers, where are you? I want to see you!  

Narrator: The two waves of white water are the bodies of the dragons.  
They illuminate the river like daylight.  
A voice comes from within the waves:  

Dragon Brothers: Young man,

You are so kind and brave.

May you always be happy, safe, and blessed!

Narrator: Xiaochen looks back at his brothers as they go away,  
tears streaming down his face, full of deep longing.  

The Dragon Brothers turn back again and again in the clouds,  
the clouds fade, the thunder grows far away.  

When spring comes and the spring rain falls gently,  
the villagers look forward to a good harvest.  

Xiaochen still tends to his ducks beneath the Crescent Mountain.  
The sky once again echoes with Liu Sanjie's song:  

"Long ago, there was a Crescent Mountain,  
Xiaochen was so kind and brave.  
He helped the Dragon return to heaven to bring rain.  
Xiaochen's heroic deeds are told everywhere..."
