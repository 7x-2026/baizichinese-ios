The Story of the Rabbits

Once upon a time, a family of rabbits lived near a large fruit tree. One day, a huge fruit fell from a branch with a crash like thunder. Terrified, the rabbits ran as fast as they could.

“Why are you running?” asked a fox.

“Because the sky is falling!” the rabbits answered. So the fox ran after them.

A few steps later, a monkey asked, “Why are you running?”

“Because the sky is falling!” said the fox. So the monkey joined them too.

Before long, a deer, a pig, a bull, a rhinoceros, an elephant, a bear, a leopard, and a tiger all heard that the sky was falling and began to run as well.

Then they met a lion. “Why are you all running?” he roared.

“Because the sky is falling!” they cried.

“How do you know?” asked the lion.

The tiger said the leopard had told him. The leopard said the bear had told him. The bear said the elephant had told him, and the elephant said the rhinoceros had told him. On and on it went, until they came to the rabbits.

“Come!” squeaked the rabbits. “We will show you where the sky fell.”

They led the lion to the fruit tree. “The sky fell right here!” they said. Just then, another huge fruit dropped from the tree.

“The sky has fallen again!” said the lion, laughing.
