The Story of the Bulls

Once, a herd of bulls grazed together in a meadow. They lived peacefully and were good friends. Some tigers hunted nearby, hoping the bulls would separate so that they could enjoy a fine meal. The tigers knew that as long as the bulls stayed together, they could not attack them.

Then the tigers had an idea. They began whispering unkind things about one of the bulls. Some birds overheard them, flew over to the herd, and perched on the bulls’ backs to repeat what they had heard.

By the time the birds flew away, the rumors had spread through the herd. The bulls looked at one another with suspicion. They began to fight and no longer grazed together.

Now the tigers knew their chance had come. They licked their lips and pounced on the scattered bulls. The bulls all became the tigers’ meal.
