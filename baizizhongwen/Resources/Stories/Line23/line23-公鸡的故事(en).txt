The Story of the Rooster

Once, a rooster lived on a farm. Every morning, when he saw the first ray of sunlight, he lifted his head and crowed loudly, “Cock-a-doodle-doo!”

Every morning, before climbing into the sky, the sun would wait for a minute and look down at the rooster.

One morning, the rooster asked the sun why it always hesitated before rising.

“I am the timekeeper of this planet,” answered the sun, “so I must always be on time. Before I rise, I wait for you to crow. Then I can be certain that morning has come.”

“But I only crow after I see the first ray of sunlight!” said the rooster.
