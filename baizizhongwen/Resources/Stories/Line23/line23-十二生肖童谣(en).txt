The Twelve Zodiac Animals

Little Rat comes first in line;
Ox lifts up his hooves in time.
Tiger turns and gives a roar;
Rabbit hops ahead once more.

Dragon and Snake swing their tails;
Horse and Sheep walk down the trails.
Clever Monkey leaps up high;
Rooster crows to greet the sky.

Dog jumps up and Pig calls out;
Rat comes round and runs about.
Twelve animals circle, one by one;
Put them in order, everyone!
