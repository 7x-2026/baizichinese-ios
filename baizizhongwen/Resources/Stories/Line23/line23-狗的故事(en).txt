The Story of the Dogs

After a good meal, Yellow Dog and Black Dog lay in the sunshine beside the wall outside the kitchen. They began a very polite conversation. They talked about all sorts of things in the world, the work they had to do, and good and evil. At last, they came to the subject of friendship.

“The greatest happiness in life,” said Black Dog, “is living with a loyal and trustworthy friend, sharing good times and hardships. Friends love and protect each other. They bring each other joy and make each other’s days happier, finding happiness in their friend’s happiness too. Could anything in the world be better? If you and I became such close friends, life would be so much nicer that we would hardly notice the time flying by.”

“Wonderful, my dear!” said Yellow Dog warmly. “Let us be friends!”

Black Dog was excited too. “Dear Yellow Dog, in the past we have fought every single day. Several times it has made me terribly sad. Why did we bother? Our owner is kind, we have plenty to eat, and there is lots of room for us. There is no reason at all to fight. Come, let us shake paws!”

“Agreed! Agreed!” cried Yellow Dog.

The two new friends immediately embraced and licked each other’s faces with delight. “Long live friendship!” they cried. “Away with quarrels, jealousy, and resentment!”

Just then, the cook tossed out a delicious-smelling bone.

Both new friends rushed at it like lightning. Their harmony melted away like a burning candle. The two “dear friends” rolled together, biting and snapping, until dog hair flew everywhere.
