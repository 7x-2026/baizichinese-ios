The Wolf and the Lamb

A wolf came to a stream and saw a lamb drinking there.

The wolf wanted very much to eat the lamb, so he deliberately found something to complain about. “You have made my drinking water dirty! What are you up to?”

The lamb was startled. “How could I make your water dirty?” he asked gently. “You are standing upstream. The water flows from you to me, not from me to you.”

“Even so, you are a bad creature!” said the wolf angrily. “I heard that last year you said unkind things about me behind my back!”

“Oh, dear Mr. Wolf, that cannot be true!” cried the poor lamb. “I had not even been born last year!”

The wolf did not want to argue any longer. Baring his teeth, he moved closer to the lamb and shouted, “You little rascal! If it was not you, it was your father. It makes no difference!” And he pounced on the lamb.

The Two Goats

Two goats met on a narrow bridge made from a single log. Neither would give way, and neither would listen to anyone’s advice. Before long, two splashes came from below the bridge. Both goats had fallen into the water.
