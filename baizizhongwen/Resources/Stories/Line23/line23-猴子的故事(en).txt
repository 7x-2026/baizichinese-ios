The Story of the Monkeys

One evening, a troop of monkeys was playing beside a well. The youngest monkey leaned over the edge and looked down. There, at the bottom of the well, was something round and shining: the moon!

“The moon has fallen into the well!” cried the little monkey.

All the monkeys gathered around and peered down. “We must pull the moon out at once!” they cried.

They climbed a tree beside the well and hung down, one from another, forming a long chain of monkeys that reached into the well. The youngest monkey was at the very bottom.

He reached the moon, but all he scooped up was a handful of water. Ripples spread across the surface, and the moon broke into pieces.

“Oh! I have broken the moon!” he cried. The other monkeys all scolded him.

Before long, the water grew still, and the moon was round and bright again, just as before. The little monkey tried once more to catch it, but once more he broke it.

Now the other monkeys hanging from the tree began to complain. Their arms and tails were aching.

Just then, the oldest monkey happened to look up and saw the moon hanging high in the sky.

“There is a new moon in the sky!” he shouted. “The old moon must have been thrown into the well!”

So all the monkeys climbed out of the well and happily admired the new moon.
