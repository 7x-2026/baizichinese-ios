The Story of the White-Headed Pig

Long ago, all the pigs raised in the villages of Liaodong were black. The people there had never seen pigs of any other color and thought that every pig in the world was black.

One year, a sow belonging to a man named Zhang San had piglets. He happily counted the newborns. “One, two…” Suddenly, he stopped, his mouth hanging open in surprise. There in the pigpen lay a little white piglet!

He hurried outside and told everyone he met, “My pig has had a white-headed piglet! A white-headed one!” People rushed to his house to see it for themselves. Everyone thought it was a marvel. Soon the news had spread through the whole village.

People excitedly discussed the rare little pig. The oldest and most widely traveled person in the village, whose words everyone respected, said, “In all my years, this is the first white-headed pig I have seen. It must be a sacred pig sent by heaven to bring us good fortune. We should take this precious pig to the capital and offer it to the emperor. Then our village will become famous throughout the land!”

Everyone agreed. They began building a carrying platform, making red flowers, and preparing gongs and drums.

A few days later, everything was ready. Several strong young men lifted the platform, which was decorated with red silk, and placed the white-headed piglet on it. Led by a capable guide, they set off joyfully toward the capital, beating gongs and drums and blowing horns.

After traveling for several days, they reached Hedong. To their astonishment, nearly every pig raised on the farms there had a white head. Such pigs were completely ordinary!

Only then did the villagers realize that the so-called sacred pig they planned to offer the emperor was just an ordinary little pig.

They looked at one another in disappointment and dismay. They put away their banners, silenced their drums, and took their little white pig home to Liaodong.
