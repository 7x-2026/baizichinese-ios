The Story of the Tiger

One day, a tiger caught a fox. The fox said grandly, “You cannot eat me. The heavenly dragon has appointed me king of the animals. If you eat me, the law of heaven will punish you.”

The tiger laughed at the fox and opened his great mouth to eat him.

“Stop!” cried the fox. “Before you eat me, let me prove that I am the king. Follow me and watch how all the other animals run away in fear when they see me.”

This seemed fair enough to the tiger. He followed the fox to a clearing in the forest, where many animals were resting. Of course, as soon as the animals saw the fox and the tiger, they all fled in terror.

“See?” said the fox. “Now leave my kingdom!”

The tiger did not realize that the animals were afraid of him, rather than the fox. He turned and ran away.
