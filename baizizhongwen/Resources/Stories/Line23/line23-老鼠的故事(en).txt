The Story of the Mouse

Once upon a time, a mouse lived beneath a rock. He was unhappy because his hole was uncomfortable, and searching for food in the hot sun was hard work.

One day, he looked up at the sky and said, “What a powerful sun! I wish I could be the sun, hanging freely above the earth.” At that very moment, the mouse became the sun. Proudly, he shone his fierce rays down on the land.

Before long, a small cloud drifted in front of him and blocked his light. “I never imagined that a cloud could be stronger than the sun,” he thought. “I wish I were a cloud.” Suddenly, he found that he had become a soft white cloud.

But soon the wind blew the cloud apart. “I never knew the wind was so powerful!” cried the mouse. “I wish I were the wind.” In an instant, he became the wind. He blew houses into the sea and ships onto the land.

The mouse was very pleased with his strength, until he came upon a rock. “Nothing can be stronger than a rock,” he thought. “I must become a rock!” And so he did.

A little later, he discovered a mouse digging a hole beneath him. He sighed. “Being a rock is very uncomfortable. I only wish I could be a mouse again.”

His final wish was granted. From that day on, the mouse never complained again.
