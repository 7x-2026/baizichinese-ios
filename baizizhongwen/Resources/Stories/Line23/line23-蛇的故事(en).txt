The Story of the Snakes

Once, two snakes lived in neighboring holes. One was called Spot and the other Stripe.

One day, Spot was hungry. He searched his hole for an egg he had just stolen, but he could not find it anywhere. He went outside to look, but found nothing there either. All he saw was his neighbor, Stripe.

“Look at him slinking around,” thought Spot. “His tongue keeps flicking in and out so greedily, and his eyes keep darting this way and that. Stripe must have stolen my egg!” But just as Spot was about to accuse him, Stripe slithered away.

Spot went back into his hole. Soon he discovered his egg tucked away in a very dark corner.

Later, he saw Stripe slithering about again, flicking his tongue and moving his eyes just as before. This time, however, none of it made Spot think that Stripe had stolen his egg.
