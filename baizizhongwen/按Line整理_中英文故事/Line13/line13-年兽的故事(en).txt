The Story of the Nian Beast — A Shadow-Puppet Play

A long, long time ago,

there was a strange beast named "Nian."

It was completely black and never made a sound.

Nian usually slept in the west mountain,

waking up once every 365 days—on New Year's Eve.

On that day, Nian would go to the eastern village to eat people.

"Year has come! Year has come!"

A black shadow appeared!

The villagers prepared seven weapons:

a knife, a dagger, a cleaver, arrows, a bow, a goad, and a spear.

"Shoot!" They launched their attack.

But all their weapons were used up,

and Nian still couldn't be hurt.

The villagers fell one by one.

At that moment, a little red child descended from the sky.

He wore red clothes and held a torch in his hands.

He went to the pile of bamboo and lit the firecrackers.

A loud "pop!" rang out.

"Crack! Crack! Crack! Crack!"

In the light and the sound of the firecrackers,

Nian ran away in fear.

"Thank you! Thank you!"

The villagers gathered around the little red child and asked:

"Why did Nian run away?"

Xiaochen told everyone:

"The Nian Beast fears red, fears bright lights, and fears loud noises.

Every New Year's Eve, as long as they set off firecrackers and hang spring couplets, everything is safe."

After speaking, he took off his red robe and gave it to every household.

The next year came again.

"Happy New Year! Happy New Year!"

The huge black shadow appeared once more.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven.

"Kill—!"

"Puff! Puff! Puff!"

House after house turned on their lights, set off firecrackers, and beat gongs and drums.

The Nian Beast ran away in fear.

From then on, it never came down the mountain again.

"New Year greetings! New Year greetings!"

The next day, on the first day of the lunar year,

the villagers went from house to house,

celebrating their safety.

At last, the Nian Beast was tamed.

People danced and sang,

welcoming the arrival of a new spring.
